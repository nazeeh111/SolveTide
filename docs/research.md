# Foundations and scope of the experiment

Research checked on 29 October 2026. This is an implementation and evaluation of established numerical ideas with a small allocation-policy experiment. It is not a claim to have invented recycling, implicit differentiation, goal-oriented compression, or forward-to-adjoint subspace transfer.

## Closest methods and what they establish

| Primary source | Existing capability | Consequence for SolveTide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [PETSc KSP manual](https://petsc.org/release/manual/ksp/) | Separates operator, preconditioner and convergence state. Exact unchanged matrices can reuse setup; changed matrices normally rebuild it, with explicit reuse available. Its convergence rule combines absolute and relative true-residual thresholds. | Factorization reuse on an exactly unchanged operator is a mandatory strong control. Operator changes must not silently retain old factors as exact solves. |
| [SciPy GCROT(m,k)](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.sparse.linalg.gcrotmk.html) | Flexible Krylov recycling carries source/image pairs across solves. Setting old image vectors to `None` causes them to be recomputed under the new operator and reorthogonalized. | The executable fixed-recycling control should use this established implementation. Refreshing operator images is established numerical hygiene, not the proposed contribution. |
| [JAX custom_linear_solve](https://docs.jax.dev/en/latest/_autosummary/jax.lax.custom_linear_solve.html) | Defines the derivative through the converged equation and a transpose solve, independently of solver iterations. The documented equation invariant is the caller's responsibility. | SolveTide exposes explicit residual acceptance and an implicit adjoint. It does not differentiate stale memory or claim a gradient from an unconverged field. JAX is a conceptual reference, not a runtime dependency. |
| [PCGBandit source](https://github.com/mkhodak/PCGBandit) and [paper, revision 4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.08765v4) | Learns solver/preconditioner configurations online from repeated PCG calls in OpenFOAM, including incomplete Cholesky and GAMG choices. | Learning from solver feedback already exists. | [G-RANS, ICML 2026](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/liao26a.html) | Learns residual-aware graph correction subspaces and uses projected numerical updates, with staged residual-distribution training. The paper reports its own accuracy and timing regime. | A learned proposal followed by numerical correction already exists. SolveTide's compact rank policy does not reproduce G-RANS. No numerical comparison to G-RANS is claimed. |
| [Sorting Krylov Recycling, ICLR 2024](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/bf331c87e29f473b610336f00fe1cb51-Abstract-Conference.html), [full paper](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/bf331c87e29f473b610336f00fe1cb51-Paper-Conference.pdf) | Sorts a known set of parameterized systems to make neighboring systems more similar before recycling; targets offline neural-operator data generation. | Interactive edits must remain in chronological order. ## The bounded experiment

For each accepted primal or adjoint solve, retain a normalized independent solution-correction direction with its role and measured solve information. Both roles compete for one fixed storage budget. A new solve assesses the retained space under the actual new matrix, rechecks numerical rank, chooses a retained rank and computes a projected starting correction. Classical iterations then solve the original system to the same tolerance used by controls.

The proposed practical question is whether a small predictor can choose how much shared memory to apply or refresh more cheaply than fixed-rank retention or a simple drift rule, after charging feature construction, matrix applications, projection, inference and retention. That question remains open until the recorded held-out study answers it. Reducing iterations alone does not establish a time saving.

The implemented update rule and feature definitions are specified in [runtime.md](runtime.md). The trained policy, data split and measured comparison belong with their versioned generated artifacts. A policy trained on ranks measured independently at one step is not automatically a sequence-optimal allocation policy; chronological validation must expose the consequences of its earlier decisions. Projection rank selection and retention allocation should be reported separately.

## Comparison requirements

Use matched tolerances and maximum work across cold classical solves, ordinary warm starts, exact-operator factor reuse, fixed GCROT, fixed solution-memory projection, unlearned role-aware memory, a drift rule, and learned memory. For multigrid, compare rebuilding and explicit reuse, and charge hierarchy setup and storage. Do not apply a nonsymmetric or changing preconditioner inside ordinary CG without satisfying its assumptions. The fixed-recycling and warm-start controls should not be renamed as the learned method.

Freeze complete held-out trajectories before fitting or tuning. Reserve mesh sizes, coefficient contrasts, boundary supports and abrupt/global edits. Keep ordering fixed, publish failures and negative cases, and count complete primal-plus-adjoint and optimization cost. A direct solve remains a useful reference on bounded systems even when it is not the fastest method. Independent finite differences must reassemble and solve perturbed equations over several step sizes; they must not reuse the differentiated expression as their reference.

## Accuracy and originality limits

For real reduced equations `A(theta) x = b(theta)` and objective derivative `g = dJ/dx`, solve `A.T lambda = g`, then evaluate each parameter derivative as `lambda.T @ (db - dA @ x)` plus any explicit parameter derivative of the objective. Prescribed-value elimination affects both `b` and `db`. These are standard discrete-adjoint equations.

A backward-error measure characterizes the stored linear equations; it does not certify a small field error without conditioning information. Conditioning estimates have their own approximation limits. Linear-solver error, finite-element discretization error and physical-model error remain different questions. This runtime does not certify physical safety, arbitrary nonlinear PDEs or derivatives across remeshing or support changes.

The defensible deliverable is a reusable, inspected sparse runtime with explicit edit validity, tested derivatives, honest end-to-end timing and an interpretable shared-memory experiment. Any claim of a new numerical method or broad acceleration would require substantially more evidence than the initial bounded study.
